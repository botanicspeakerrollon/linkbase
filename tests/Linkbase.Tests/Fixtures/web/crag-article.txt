Five Quiet Crags for a Long Weekend

This is an invented article used only for testing. Every crag and route below is fictional, and any likeness to a real place is an accident of the naming.

Zzq Sandstone Edge

Zzq Sandstone Edge is a gritty escarpment in the Zzq Valley, in England. The rock is sandstone and it dries fast after rain. It sits at 54.1234, -2.5678 and gets the morning sun.

The classic here is Quiet Harbour, E1 5b, a 25m line with two hard moves past a thin flake that keeps your attention until the very end. Next door, Paper Boats is VS 4c and takes the obvious crack for 20m. Beginners should head for Long Way Round, HS 4a.

Larkfield

Everyone knows Larkfield, but few people go in midweek. Slab Theory 7b+ remains a must, and the newer Zzq Overhang 7a gives steep sport climbing on good holds, with two pitches.
